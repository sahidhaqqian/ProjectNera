# Pet sound sources

All ten bundled pet cues are by **Kenney**, licensed **CC0 1.0**. The original
pack licenses are retained alongside this file. These assets permit commercial
use, modification and redistribution without mandatory attribution.

Files are unmodified copies from the creator's downloads. Nera applies quiet
per-cue playback gains, the existing master volume/mute, semantic cooldowns,
and a limit of four simultaneous players. Every cue lasts less than one second.
No voice lines, music, or continuous walking/idle audio were added.
